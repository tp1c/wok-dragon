Our Legacy

20 Years of Fire, Flavor & Heart

For more than two decades, Wokdragon has been a cherished neighborhood landmark in the heart of Atlanta, delivering comforting warmth, authentic wok-tossed dishes, and contemporary Chinese craftsmanship.

🔥

The Art of Wok-Fired Cooking

Every stir-fry is tossed at intense temperatures over roaring flames, locking in crisp vegetable textures, seared meats, and our signature smoky wok aroma.

🥭

Innovative Signature Recipes
