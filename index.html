Our Legacy

20 Years of Fire, Flavor & Heart

For more than two decades, Wokdragon has been a cherished neighborhood landmark in the heart of Atlanta, delivering comforting warmth, authentic wok hei, and contemporary Chinese craftsmanship.

🔥

The Mastery of Wok Hei

Every stir-fry is tossed at intense temperatures over roaring flames, locking in crisp vegetable textures, seared meats, and our signature smoky wok aroma.

🥭

Innovative Signature Recipes

From our crowd-favorite sweet & tangy Mango Chicken and rich Bourbon Chicken to golden Dragon Fries, we marry classic Chinese recipes with vibrant contemporary tastes.

📍

Two Convenient Atlanta Hubs

Whether you're shopping at Lenox Square Mall food court or picking up dinner at Chamblee Tucker Rd, delicious hot meals are always minutes away.
